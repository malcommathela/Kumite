The craft

We roast to a profile, not a colour

Light enough to keep the fruit, long enough to lose the grass. Every lot is cupped as a sample, again mid-roast, and once more a week later.

If a coffee needs milk and sugar to be pleasant, we don’t sell it.

The source

One farm at a time

We buy small lots through a single importer and print the farm, the varietal and the harvest on the bag.

When a lot runs out it comes off the shelf. We don’t substitute, even when the replacement is cheaper.

The method

A recipe you can repeat

  1. 01

    Dose

    20g of coffee, ground medium-fine. Enough for a mug and a second cup.

  2. 02

    Bloom

    60g of water at 94°C. Wait forty seconds. The bed should look like wet sand.

  3. 03

    Pour

    Top up to 320g in two slow circles. Drawdown should finish around 2:45.
